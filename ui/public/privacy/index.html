AI ROI Calculator (/roi/)

  • The numbers you enter stay in your browser. They are not sent to any server and are not included in any pixel event. The "Save your results" link stores them in the URL hash only, which browsers do not send to servers.
  • If you enter your email to see your results, we do not store it. It is passed only to Substack in the link that opens the Work3 newsletter subscribe page, where Substack's privacy policy applies.
  • We use the Meta pixel (ID 1473589161254534) to measure our ads: page view, calculator start and completion, and email submission. No inputs or email are included. Advanced matching is off. Where a consent banner is shown, the pixel loads only if you accept.
  • Your browser's session storage keeps the ad parameters in the URL (such as utm_* and fbclid) so they can be passed to Substack.

Substack: substack.com/privacy
